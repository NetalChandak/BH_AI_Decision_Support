# Growth factors / scenario drivers

| Driver | Direction of effect | Conservative | Base | Accelerated | Evidence basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public transport network delivery | PT share | Slow | Planned/base | Fast | NoRA mobility roadmap and network strategy |
| Transit accessibility | PT share | Moderate | Strong | Very strong | TOD + accessibility-led planning |
| Greenfield/TOD effect | PT + sustainable modes | Low | Medium | High | Explicit NoRA rationale |
| Density / mixed use | Internalisation + sustainable modes | Low | Medium | High | NoRA urban-form rationale |
| Parking / TDM | Reduces car dependence | Weak | Moderate | Strong | NoRA parking-policy discussion |
| Behavioural adoption | PT + active modes | Slow | Moderate | Fast | Analytical assumption |
| Development phasing | All outcomes | Delayed | Base | Accelerated | Project planning context |
| Population / trip demand | Demand scale | Lower | Mid | Higher | 3.0M ±7.5% source scenario |
| Technology / new mobility | Sustainable access | Low | Moderate | High | NoRA future-ready mobility objectives |

## Important

These are **scenario drivers**, not all are measured historical growth rates. Where an empirical coefficient is unavailable, the factor is represented qualitatively and translated into a bounded range.
