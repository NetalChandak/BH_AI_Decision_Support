# Historical / current baseline

## Available baseline

The NoRA Urban Framework describes Riyadh as heavily car-dependent and presents public transport at approximately **9% of trips** in the mobility benchmarking context.

This is the principal current baseline used in the working analysis.

## Baseline interpretation

The baseline is **not** a NoRA observed baseline because NoRA is a greenfield development with limited existing transport infrastructure. It is a Riyadh-wide contextual baseline.

## Implication for modelling

A direct linear extrapolation from 9% to the long-term NoRA target would be inappropriate because NoRA is explicitly intended to have a different urban form and mobility system. The analysis therefore treats the 9% figure as a starting context and separately accounts for:

- greenfield development;
- higher density;
- transit-oriented development;
- early integrated transit/accessibility planning;
- planned metro/Q-Express/bus integration;
- parking and travel-demand-management measures;
- behavioural adoption and delivery risk.

## Data gap

The source pack does not provide a sufficiently complete observed NoRA time series to statistically estimate a 2030 modal-share CAGR. The resulting 2030 estimate is therefore a scenario-based working estimate, not a statistically fitted forecast.
