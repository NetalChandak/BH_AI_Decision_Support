## Source classification

**Direct source fact:** explicitly stated in the source.

**Planning target/KPI:** an aspiration, benchmark or KPI stated by the project; not necessarily a 2030 observed outcome.

**Modelled value:** created by the analysis from evidence + assumptions.

**Benchmark:** a comparable project's target or observed value; not directly transferable to NoRA.
