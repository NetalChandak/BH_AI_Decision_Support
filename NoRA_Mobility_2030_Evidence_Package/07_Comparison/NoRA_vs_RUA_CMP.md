# Independent NoRA estimate vs. RUA/CMP benchmark

## Purpose

The RUA/CMP figures are used as a **comparable planning benchmark**, not as an input to the NoRA forecast. This avoids circular reasoning.

| Metric | Independent NoRA 2030 working estimate | RUA/CMP planning target | Difference / observation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| External PT | **25–30%** | **60%** | RUA target is materially higher than the independent transitional NoRA estimate |
| External car | **70–75%** | **40%** | Residual external-mode comparison |
| Internal sustainable modes | **~45%** | **100%** (54% walking + 30% PRT + 16% micromobility) | RUA assumes a much more fully sustainable internal environment |

## Important comparability caveats

1. RUA is a different development in Makkah with a different visitor/resident/travel-demand context.
2. RUA's 60/40 and 54/30/16 values are stated modal-split planning targets, not observed 2030 results.
3. The RUA 100% internal sustainable figure is the arithmetic sum of its three stated internal modes.
4. The NoRA estimates are independent working estimates derived from NoRA/Riyadh evidence and explicit assumptions.
5. The comparison therefore indicates **difference in ambition/assumption**, not proof that either number is correct or incorrect.
