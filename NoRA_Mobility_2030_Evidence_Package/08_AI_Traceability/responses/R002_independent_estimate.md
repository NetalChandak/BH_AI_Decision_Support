# R002 — Independent estimate

The analysis proposed an independent scenario-based 2030 estimate using:

- Riyadh current PT baseline;
- Riyadh planning trajectory;
- NoRA long-term mobility ambitions;
- greenfield/TOD and density effects;
- public transport infrastructure;
- parking/TDM;
- behavioural adoption;
- development phasing;
- population/demand context.

The important methodological decision was to **not use the RUA/CMP 60% PT target as an input to the NoRA forecast**. RUA was retained as a comparison benchmark to avoid circular reasoning.
