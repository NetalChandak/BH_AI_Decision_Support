# Sensitivity method

Sensitivity is applied to the **drivers**, not only to the final percentage.

A lower bound represents combinations of:

- delayed transit delivery;
- weaker accessibility;
- slower behavioural adoption;
- slower development activation;
- weaker parking/TDM effects.

An upper bound represents combinations of:

- strong transit accessibility;
- accelerated network delivery;
- high TOD effectiveness;
- stronger parking/TDM;
- faster behavioural adoption;
- higher internal trip capture.

The result should be interpreted as a **decision range**, not a probability distribution.
