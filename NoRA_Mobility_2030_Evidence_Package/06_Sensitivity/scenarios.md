# 2030 sensitivity scenarios

| Metric | Conservative | Base / working | Accelerated |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Public transport mode share | ~19–25% | **25–30%** | ~30–35% |
| Internal sustainable modes | ~35–45% | **~45%** | ~50–55% |
| Internal trips within NoRA | ~50–55% | **~55–60%** | ~60–65% |
| Population context | ~2.5M | ~3.0M | ~3.5M |

## Interpretation

### Conservative

Slower network delivery, weaker behavioural response, slower development activation and weaker parking/TDM effects.

### Base / working

Moderate network delivery, meaningful TOD/accessibility benefits, moderate behavioural adoption and moderate demand-management effects.

### Accelerated

Strong transit accessibility, faster infrastructure delivery, strong parking/TDM implementation, faster adoption of active/public transport and stronger internalisation.

The ranges are designed to expose uncertainty rather than imply statistical confidence intervals.
