# Scope and boundaries

## In scope

- 2030 working estimates
- Public transport mode share
- Internal sustainable-mode share
- Internalisation within NoRA
- Population as a demand context
- Conservative/base/accelerated scenarios
- Comparison with stated NoRA and RUA/CMP targets
- AI prompt/output traceability

## Out of scope / not yet available

- A formal four-step travel-demand model
- Calibration against observed NoRA travel data
- Independent estimation of 2030 walking, PRT and micromobility shares individually
- Detailed network assignment or capacity modelling
- Claiming that a working estimate is an approved target

## Validation gate

The estimates should be revisited when the detailed NoRA travel-demand assessment, mobility network/capacity modelling and approved mobility plan become available.
