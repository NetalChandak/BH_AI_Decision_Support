# Limitations and open evidence gaps

1. The supplied source pack does not contain a complete observed NoRA historical modal-share time series.
2. The NoRA 2030 estimate is not statistically calibrated.
3. Riyadh's current ~9% PT share is contextual and is not equivalent to a NoRA baseline.
4. Long-term targets are not automatically 2030 forecasts.
5. Population is a planning scenario and development phasing affects actual 2030 activity.
6. Detailed public-transport supply and capacity testing is still part of the mobility-planning roadmap.
7. Internal walking, PRT and micromobility shares cannot be separated robustly from the supplied source alone.
8. Behavioural response to parking and demand-management measures is uncertain.
9. The sensitivity ranges are decision ranges, not confidence intervals or probabilities.
10. The analysis should be updated when the detailed demand/capacity model and approved mobility plan are available.
